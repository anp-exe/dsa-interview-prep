## How to write one up

Copy the [problem template](../template.md) and keep the same four steps: plan, pseudocode, first attempt, optimise. Keep the failed attempt on the page, that is the part that is worth rereading.

!!! plan "Rule for this section"

    Twenty five minutes on a problem. If it is still not moving, read the editorial, then close it and rewrite the solution from scratch the next day without looking. Reading a solution teaches nothing. Reproducing one teaches a lot.
